1. TERMINOLOGY
- The Strata Titles Amendment Act 2018 came into force on 1 May 2020. VERIFIED [ACT]
- "Strata company": the body corporate created when the scheme is registered (s3, s14). All lot owners are members. VERIFIED [ACT]
- "Council": "the governing body of a strata company" (s3). It carries out the company's functions, subject to ordinary resolution, and one member is chairperson (s135). The Act never says "council of owners", so use "council". VERIFIED [ACT]
- Strata managers are NOT licensed in WA. Never say "licensed strata manager". VERIFIED [ACT][REGS]
  - They are authorised under s143 and need a written contract that meets the s145 minimums.
  - They need criminal record checks (regs 92-93), education (reg 95) and experience (reg 97B, added 2025: principals 2 years, managers 1 year).
  - They need PI insurance of at least $1m per claim (reg 98), trust accounts (s148, reg 101) and an annual return to Landgate (reg 102).
  - Licensing came up in the 2025 five-year review. Outcome UNVERIFIED.
- Scheme by-laws come in two types (s3). VERIFIED [ACT]
  - Governance by-laws: governance, development, exclusive use, architectural rules. Default set is Schedule 1, which includes by-law 1 (the owner maintains and repairs the lot).
  - Conduct by-laws: how occupiers behave, use of lots and common property, services, safety/security, disputes. Default set is Schedule 2.
  - Amendment thresholds (governance: resolution without dissent; conduct: special resolution) are UNVERIFIED (search snippet only).
- Funds. VERIFIED
  - Administrative fund: mandatory (s100(1)).
  - Reserve fund: mandatory for a "designated strata company", meaning 10+ lots or a building replacement cost over $5m (s100(7), reg 79). Optional otherwise.
  - 10 year plan: designated companies only, revised at least every 5 years (s100(2A)).
- Levies: the statutory term is "contributions" (s3). They are split by unit entitlement unless the by-laws say otherwise. Arrears carry 11% p.a. simple interest (reg 78). A new owner is jointly and severally liable for arrears (s100(6)). VERIFIED
- Budget: approved at the AGM by ordinary resolution (s102). Improvements to common property costing more than (number of lots x $500) need a special resolution (reg 80). VERIFIED
- Meetings. VERIFIED
  - AGM: once every 12 months and no more than 15 months apart. The agenda must include council election, accounts, and insurance certificates and schedules (s127).
  - EGM: any other general meeting. The council must call one on written request from owners holding 25% or more of unit entitlements (s128).
  - Notice: 14 days to owners and first mortgagees (s129).
- Common property: the part of the parcel not in a lot (s10). In a built strata scheme the default lot boundary is the inner surface of the walls, the upper surface of the floor and the under surface of the ceiling (s3(2)). Survey-strata plans mark common property explicitly. VERIFIED [ACT][FORM]
- Disputes go to the State Administrative Tribunal (SAT). VERIFIED
- Do not use "owners corporation", "strata committee", "capital works fund", "sinking fund", "council of owners" or "s184 certificate". None appears in the WA Act. VERIFIED (text search of the Act). That they come from NSW/VIC/QLD is UNVERIFIED.

2. TRIAGE TAXONOMY (practice is UNVERIFIED; legal points are cited)
Responsibility:
- The owner maintains and repairs the lot (Sch 1 by-law 1). VERIFIED
- The strata company maintains, repairs and replaces common property, its fixtures and lifts, whatever the cause (s91(1)(c)). VERIFIED
- A scheme's registered by-laws can change this, so always check them.
- Pipes (rule of thumb, UNVERIFIED): a pipe or fitting inside a lot that serves only that lot (taps, flexi-hoses, appliances, shower) is the owner's. A pipe in the slab, a riser or a common wall, or one serving several lots, is usually the strata company's. Check the plan and by-laws.

Categories and allocation:
1. EMERGENCY (P1, immediate): water actively escaping, fire/smoke, gas, sparking electrics, structural danger, someone trapped in a lift, building left unsecured, storm damage that opens the roof, water entering another lot, injury, break-in. Allocate: 000 if anyone is in danger, after-hours contractor, strata manager, and the insurer if there is damage.
2. URGENT (P2, same or next business day): slow leak, blocked sewer or stormwater, common-area lighting out, car-park door stuck, fire panel fault, loss of shared services. Allocate: strata manager and contractor.
3. ROUTINE (P3, 2-5 business days): gardens, cleaning, gutters, cosmetic repairs, quotes. Allocate: strata manager; council approval if above the spending delegation.
4. INSURANCE: building and common-property claims go to the strata manager and the strata company's insurer. Contents and the owner's own liability go to the owner's insurer.
5. LEVIES/ACCOUNTS: notices, arrears, payment plans, contact updates. Allocate: accounts.
6. SALES/SETTLEMENT: s110 certificates and s107 requests. Allocate: accounts. A 14-day statutory deadline applies.
7. BY-LAW/CONDUCT: noise, parking, pets, rubbish, smoking, short-stay. Allocate: strata manager, then council, then SAT.
8. OWNER APPROVALS: renovations, structural work, air-con, pets. Allocate: council or a general meeting.
9. GOVERNANCE: meetings, motions, proxies, minutes, records requests. Allocate: strata manager.
10. CONTRACTORS: quotes, invoices, access, compliance documents. Allocate: strata manager or accounts.
11. TENANTS/PROPERTY MANAGERS: tenants are not members, so send lot issues to the landlord or property manager. Conduct by-laws still bind occupiers.
12. LEGAL/SAT/FORMAL COMPLAINT: escalate to a senior manager.

3. EMERGENCY HANDLING
Law. VERIFIED
- The strata company may enter a lot without notice in an emergency, using reasonable force if needed (s95(2), s65(3)(a), s65(6)).
- Required insurance (s97, reg 9, [SCA]):
  - All insurable assets, including buildings, insured to replacement value.
  - Cover against fire, storm and tempest (not sea, flood or erosion), lightning, explosion and earthquake.
  - Public liability of at least $10m.
  - Insurance payouts must go to repairing or rebuilding.
- Owners insure their own liability and contents. In survey-strata schemes they also insure the buildings on their lot (s97 notes; [SCA]).
- Neither the Act nor the regulations says who pays the insurance excess. It depends on the policy and the by-laws, so never promise.

Contact numbers (VERIFIED from secondary sources):
- 000: life-threatening emergencies
- SES 132 500: storm damage
- Western Power faults: 13 13 51
- Water Corporation: 13 13 75

First reply (practice, UNVERIFIED):
(1) If there is danger, fire, smoke, gas or sparking, call 000 and get out.
(2) For a leak, if it is safe: turn off the lot stop-cock or the building main, switch off power if water is near electrics, and contain the water.
(3) Tell them an after-hours contractor has been sent to make the area safe.
(4) Ask them to take photos/video with the time noted and to keep damaged items.
(5) Ask them to warn neighbours and tell their property manager or tenant.
(6) Building damage is claimed through us with the strata company's insurer. Contents go through their own insurer. Excess depends on the policy and by-laws.
(7) Give a reference number and the next update time. Do not admit liability.

Other situations:
- Lift entrapment: use the lift alarm or the 24/7 number in the lift car, call 000 if there is a medical issue, and never force the doors.
- Storm: make safe; call the SES if the roof is breached.
- Security: police if a crime is in progress, then a locksmith or glazier.

4. BUYER/SETTLEMENT DISCLOSURE (VERIFIED unless marked)
- Before the contract (s156), the seller gives the buyer the "Precontractual Disclosure Statement to the Buyer" (Approved Form 2022-938). Part A is general information, Part B is about the specific lot. It can be given electronically if the buyer consents (reg 104). [ACT][FORM][REGS]
- Do NOT call "Form 28/29" current; they are pre-2020 forms. How they were merged into the new form is UNVERIFIED.
- After the contract (s157), the seller must notify type 1 and type 2 variations. The buyer may delay settlement by up to 15 working days (s158) or avoid the contract (s159-162). Disputes go to SAT.
- s107 requests must be in writing. They can come from:
  - a member;
  - a buyer who has ENTERED a contract;
  - a mortgagee;
  - a class of person named in the regulations;
  - or someone any of these authorise in writing (e.g. a settlement agent).
  A buyer who has not yet signed needs the seller's written authority.
- Maximum fees (reg 86; add 10% if GST applies):
  - s108 contact information: $10
  - s109 inspection: $1 ($100 if made by an authorised person)
  - s110(1) certificate (management contract, insurance, unregistered dealings): $140
  - s110(2) certificate (contributions, arrears, interest): $140
  - An application is not validly made until the fee is paid.
- Deadlines: s108 and s110 within 14 days. For a s109 inspection, a time must be agreed within 3 days; otherwise the strata company sets one between 9am and 5pm within 10 days. Penalty for missing these: $3,000. A s110 certificate is conclusive evidence for the buyer.
- Copies (reg 88): $50 by email; photocopies $50 for the first 5 pages plus $1 per extra page. Taking photos is free and cannot be refused (reg 87).

5. DOCUMENTS KEPT PER SCHEME
Required by law. VERIFIED [ACT s104-105; REGS 10, 83, 84]; electronic records are allowed:
- Scheme notice, plan, registered by-laws, schedule of unit entitlements, pending amendments.
- Meeting minutes and notices: 7 years. Resolutions: 20 years for special, unanimous and without-dissent resolutions, 7 years for others. Accounts and correspondence: 7 years.
- Contracts (management, insurance, service, infrastructure) and leases or licences over common property.
- Key documents: developer contracts and plans, as-constructed plans, Building Act completion notices, defect documents, water-service agreements, the 10 year plan.
- Fund balances, budget, current levy resolution, termination proposals, roll of owners, and the insurance certificates presented at the AGM (s127).
